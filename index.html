01 / Why we exist

Question the culture.
Not the celebrity.

We challenge blind celebrity worship, double standards and online fan wars. We celebrate creativity and achievement without treating fame as superiority.

Our manifesto

Enjoy the work. Celebrate the talent. But never trade your judgement, dignity or individuality for someone else's popularity.

We don't choose sides, name celebrities or invite personal attacks. Our principles apply equally to everyone — including us.

No favourites. No double standards. No blind worship.
“If your principles change depending on who's involved, are they really principles?”

04 / Community

Disagree thoughtfully.
Think freely.

A community should be a place for reflection, not a battlefield for fan rivalries.

Our conversation code

  1. Challenge opinions, not identities.
  2. No celebrity names or personal attacks in our editorial content.
  3. Respect disagreement; no harassment or fan-war bait.
  4. Use the same standards for everyone.
  5. Stay curious. Be willing to reconsider.

Public comments, member accounts and moderation will be introduced when a secure community system is connected.
